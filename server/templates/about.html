About

Paste Link Get Video

Paste Link Get Video is a fast, clean downloader for public video content. Paste a link, fetch available formats, and save MP4 or MP3 for offline viewing.

What we do

  • Fetch publicly available video info from a pasted URL.
  • Let you pick quality and download MP4 video or MP3 audio.
  • Require no login and store nothing about you.

What we don't do

  • No private, login-only, or paid content downloading.
  • No hosting of media files — files pass through only for your download.
  • No affiliation with YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, or Instagram.
