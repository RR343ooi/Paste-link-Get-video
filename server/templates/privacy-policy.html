Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Paste Link Get Video requires no login and is designed to store as little as possible.

What we collect

  • URLs you submit — used only to fetch video info and prepare your download, then discarded from active processing.
  • Basic server logs (e.g. IP address, user agent) for security and abuse prevention.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no tracking profiles, no sale of personal data.
  • No permanent storage of your pasted links tied to your identity.

Cookies

We do not set advertising cookies. Strictly necessary technical storage may be used by the browser or hosting provider.

Third parties

When you submit a URL, our server contacts the source platform to retrieve public video metadata. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.
