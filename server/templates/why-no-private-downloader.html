Why No Private Downloader

Public content only — by design.

Paste Link Get Video intentionally does not support private, login-only, or paid videos. That is not a missing feature — it is a safety and legal choice.

Reasons

  • Privacy: private videos belong to their owner and invited viewers only. Bypassing that would violate their privacy.
  • Security: private downloading would require your login cookies or credentials — we never ask for them.
  • Copyright: most private or paid content is not licensed for downloading. We stay on the public, lawful side.

If a video needs a login, membership, or private link to view, please don't try to download it here — it will fail by design.
